gdp-apply

Copy a CASA calibration table and write selected GDP samples and flags into it.

Usage

script/gdp-apply --mode gain --scan 17 --datacolumn casa --output-casa-table /path/to/output.g
script/gdp-apply --mode bandpass --scan 18 --datacolumn model --antenna "[0-29]" --chans 800 3000 --use-flags --output-casa-table /path/to/output.b

Behavior

--output-casa-table is required and must not already exist. GDP copies the input CASA table, replaces selected samples in its calibration data column with casa (default) or model gains NPZ samples, then writes a selected sidecar into CASA FLAG. If the requested/highest flag sidecar is absent, GDP prints a warning and writes the data without applying flags.
